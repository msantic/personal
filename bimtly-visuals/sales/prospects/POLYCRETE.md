## Company Snapshot

Canadian-founded manufacturer of Insulated Concrete Forms (ICF) with an international licensee network. Original product FLEX 850 (1988); current lines include P2, V1 Series, and the flagship **Big Block** (launched April 2026) — a 3× oversized commercial-grade ICF with hinged steel crossties, 4-hour fire rating, R-37 insulation, and core widths 5⅝"–24". Sells to architects/engineers, general contractors, mid-rise and mixed-use developers, residential builders, and a certified installer network. Already digitally mature: operates **XpressWall**, a proprietary service that digitizes architectural plans into factory-precut, numbered ICF kits.

## The Hook — Why BIMTLY fits Polycrete

Polycrete sits at the sweet spot of our ICP: a manufacturer of complex configurable physical products with a multi-country footprint, already digitally literate (they built XpressWall), but with the sales-facing architect/specifier layer undeveloped. The **Big Block** launch is a textbook case for a 3D configurator: multiple core widths, R-values, heights, corner angles, reinforcement options — exactly what our parametric engine is built to render and price live.

**Fit reasons:**
1. **Configurable product with clear axes** — cores (5⅝"–24"), R-values (up to R-37), wall heights, corner angles, reinforcement variants, buck frames.
2. **International licensee network** — 15+ countries, multiple manufacturing entities (Canada, UK, Norway, Japan); white-label dealer/country portal is a direct structural fit.
3. **Digitally literate but asymmetric** — XpressWall covers plans → factory side brilliantly. The upstream specifier experience (architects picking a system, getting a ballpark, downloading a Revit family) isn't there.
4. **Specific signal** — Big Block launch April 2026, visible at IBS 2026 (Orlando, Feb 17–19).
