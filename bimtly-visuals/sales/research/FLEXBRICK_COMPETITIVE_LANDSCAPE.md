# Flexbrick — Competitive Landscape & Digital Maturity

**Vertical**: Flexible / prefab / parametric ceramic-brick & terracotta façade systems
**Anchor company**: Flexbrick (ES) — ceramic elements bonded to steel mesh, deployed as façades, pergolas, pavements, screens
**Compiled**: 2026-04-26
**Purpose**: (1) competitor map for Flexbrick deal context; (2) prospect-mining for BIMTLY in adjacent firms

---

## What Flexbrick Actually Competes With

Flexbrick is **not** competing with ordinary brick manufacturers. Its real competitive set is:

- Rainscreen terracotta systems
- Brick veneer / brick-slip panels
- Architectural metal screens & sunshades
- Prefab brick cladding
- Parametric façade materials

Positioning: a flexible architectural skin using ceramic elements on steel mesh — sold to architects for façades, pergolas, pavements, and curved/parametric geometries.

## Flexbrick Strategic Profile

**Moat**:
- IP around ceramic-textile construction
- Strong visual identity
- Useful for parametric / curved architecture
- Faster install vs. traditional masonry

**Weaknesses**:
- Niche market
- Requires architect education & specification
- Premium pricing
- Scale gated by distribution network

## Digital Maturity Across the Vertical

Most façade/material manufacturers are still operating at **~2015 digital maturity**. Their typical "configurator" stack:

- Color dropdown selectors
- PDF spec sheets
- Sample-request forms
- Project galleries
- Revit / BIM object downloads
- Contact-the-rep workflows

What's almost entirely **absent** in this niche:

- Live 3D product scenes
- AR building previews
- Instant pricing / parametric quoting
- AI-assisted façade design
- Architect collaboration portals
- Multilingual self-service sales

### What competitors actually have today

| Company | 3D Configurator | Web Configurator | BIM/Revit | Notes |
|---------|----------------|------------------|-----------|-------|
| **NBK Architectural Terracotta** | Partial | Partial | Yes | Strong customization tools (shapes, colors, glazes, textures, corners, machining) but more design-inspiration / product-selector than live 3D |
| **Shildan** | No | Limited | Yes | Excellent project gallery; sales-assisted specification, no public live configurator |
| **Wienerberger** | Some tools | Yes | Yes | Strongest digital player in masonry — brick selectors, façade inspiration, calculators, regional planning. Still not SaaS-grade 3D |
| **Terreal** | Limited | Limited | Yes | Product selectors, not configurators |
| **Hunter Douglas Architectural** | Better than brick firms | Yes | Yes | Strongest digital product selection in the screen segment |

### Digital-maturity ranking (vertical-wide)

1. Hunter Douglas Architectural
2. Wienerberger
3. NBK Architectural Terracotta
4. Terreal
5. Shildan

**No company in this niche has a world-class browser-based 3D configurator yet.** Open territory.

## Implications for BIMTLY

Flexbrick-adjacent firms are textbook BIMTLY ICP:

- Architectural materials manufacturers
- High product complexity (color × shape × glaze × pattern × geometry)
- Specification-driven sales (architect → contractor → project)
- Multi-country distributor / licensee networks
- Premium positioning, weak digital experience

What BIMTLY can offer that none of them currently ship:

- Real-time 3D façade configurator
- Pattern / parametric façade generator
- BIM export from configured output
- Architect specification portal with shareable scenes
- AR building preview
- Multilingual self-service quoting & lead capture
- Global dealer / licensee back-office

The "who has configurators?" question is the wrong frame. The right frame:

> **Which 500 manufacturers in this segment badly need one?**
